>
> **Hellman v. Craven (No. 26-97)** is an Institute for Justice case challenging Massachusetts' "Place Restriction" ([603 CMR 28.03(1)(e)(3)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26-97/416853/20260720111305565_Petition%20for%20a%20Writ%20of%20Certiorari%20Final.pdf)), which bars children with disabilities from receiving state-funded special education services on the premises of private schools where their parents enrolled them. The First Circuit ([opinion, Mar. 20, 2026](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca1/25-1417/25-1417-2026-03-20.html)) applied rational basis review and upheld the regulation as implementing the Massachusetts anti-aid (Blaine) amendment. The question presented: **whether the unconstitutional conditions doctrine applies to the *Pierce v. Society of Sisters* right to send one's child to private school.**
