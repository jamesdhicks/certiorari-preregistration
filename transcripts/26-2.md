> ## Case Summary
>
> **No. 26-2, Independent Specialty Ins. Co. v. Lower Cameron Hospital Service District** is a Hurricane Laura coverage dispute in which domestic surplus-lines insurers sought to compel arbitration under the New York Convention. The Fifth Circuit summarily affirmed the denial of arbitration (Mar. 31, 2026), relying on its published decision in *Town of Vinton v. Indian Harbor Ins. Co.*, 161 F.4th 282 (5th Cir. 2025), which held that (1) a "separate contract" allocation endorsement means no foreign party is on the domestic insurers' arbitration agreements, and (2) **state law** (Louisiana's, which bars insurance arbitration per *Police Jury of Calcasieu Parish*) governs whether a nonsignatory may use equitable estoppel to enforce a Convention arbitration agreement — the question expressly reserved in *GE Energy v. Outokumpu* (2020). The petition alleges a 4–1 circuit split (1st, 2d, 4th, 9th applying federal common law).
